# Participation and computing consent

The final legal wording must be reviewed before public launch. The application must communicate at minimum:

- The participant voluntarily allows MINE-ME to use a portion of the device's computing resources while the supported browser session is active.
- The participant chooses the mining intensity and can pause or stop at any time.
- Compute may increase CPU use, electricity/data use, heat, fan activity and device/battery wear.
- Browser tabs can be throttled or suspended by the browser or operating system; continuous background mining is not promised.
- All pool proceeds belong to MINE-ME/platform ownership. Participants receive no revenue share and have no withdrawable individual balance.
- Device-level contribution figures are internal attribution/analytics only, not participant earnings.
- Mining does not start unless the participant has affirmatively accepted the current version of these terms.
